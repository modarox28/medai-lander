What MedIA Suite is and isn't

MedIA Suite is a clinical decision support tool in development. It is not a certified medical device, has no health registration with INVIMA (Colombia's regulator) and does not replace the judgment of healthcare staff. AI suggestions can be wrong: the decision about each patient always belongs to whoever is treating them.

Demo mode uses fictitious data. Don't enter real patient data in the demo.

Privacy

This page doesn't collect personal data. It has no forms and no sign-up. The "Email me" button opens your email app; if you write, your address and message are only used to reply to you.

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data about the visit (such as the IP address) for security; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

If the visit counter (GoatCounter) is turned on, it only counts visits in aggregate, without cookies and without identifying anyone.

How data is handled inside the app (healthcare staff and patients) is described in the MedIA Suite data processing policy (in Spanish), under Colombia's Law 1581 of 2012. You can ask to access, update, correct or delete your data by writing to mdq2804@gmail.com.

Cookies

This page doesn't use cookies, first- or third-party, and stores nothing in your browser. Fonts, images and scripts are served from the same site, with no advertising or tracking services. That's why there's no cookie banner.

The MedIA Suite app doesn't use tracking cookies either; it only stores what it needs to work in your browser (session, preferences and data for working offline), as its policy explains.
